## Please fix before ordering

**1. ADS7830 logic levels (two nets).** VDD and REF are now both +5 V, which
fixes the REF-above-VDD problem but creates another: the FPGA's I²C is 3.3 V
and the ADS7830's VIH is 0.7 × VDD = 3.5 V at 5 V, so SCL/SDA highs are out
of spec. Go the other way: **VDD = +3.3 V**, leave REF as an output with
100 nF to ground (internal 2.5 V reference), and put the volume pot across
+3.3 V so the wiper stays in range.

**2. Mounting holes.** There are none — the only 3.2 mm drills are the DE-15
bracket pair, and the 1.6 mm pair is D4's leads. A 267 × 199 mm 4-layer board
with a 40-pin socket and eight MTA headers needs standoffs; four corner holes
at least.

## Worth doing on this spin (they will show up on first hookup)

**3. THS7374 input level.** The 510/1k/2k ladder still feeds the amp input
with no shunt, so full-scale is 3.3 V, doubled to 6.6 V against a ~2.3 V
linear input range — the top of the range clips. A **75 Ω to ground at each
of U13's three inputs** brings full-scale to ~0.7 V, which is what the 2×
gain and the 75 Ω series resistor are designed around. Also note the VGA
connector and J2 hang off the *same* 75 Ω, so with both plugged in the level
halves; separate resistors per connector if both stay.

**4. Audio.** The board has no amplifier. On the real cabinet, J3 pins 7/9
(and DoT's 6/7 and 9/10) are the *speaker terminals* of the sound board's own
amp — a cabinet speaker plugged into this J3 gets a 2 kΩ-source line-level
signal and will be near-silent. Either add an LM386/TPA-class stage per
channel (the spec assumed one on the 12 V rail) or document that an external
amp is required.

On the Discs of Tron 10-pin J3: per the archived pinout, 6/7 are the + and −
of speaker 1 and 9/10 of speaker 2. The board puts `AUD_PWM_R1` on 6 and
`AUD_PWM_L1` on 7 — two different channels across one speaker. DoT has two
speakers, each bridge-driven, not four. If you want the second pair
(`AUD_PWM_L2/R2` on J10 pins 1 and 5) to survive, it needs an RTL decision
on our side too — nothing generates a second stereo pair today, and those
balls currently carry video bit 0.

## Small / for the record

- No cap on the regulator side of L1/L2 (U24/U25 IN nets are just inductor +
  module pin). RECOM wants the input cap at the pin; 10 µF after each
  inductor.
- No I²C pull-ups on `ADC_SDA/SCL`. FPGA internal pull-ups will do for the
  prototype.
- `Start 1/2/3 Lamp` nets on U10 go to no connector (ERC flags them).
- J5.20/21 meter polarity is the reverse of the table (20 = Return, 21 =
  Meter 1). Harmless for a bare coil; check how the harness uses "Return".
- **J1 pin 18 = +12 V and 6/7/10/20 = GND: I could not verify these positions**
  — the archived table lists J1's functions but not its 20 pin numbers.
  Please check against the MCR power-supply manual; a wrong +12 V pin on the
  .156" connector is the one mistake that destroys parts.
- J0 (the 2×20 socket) is top-side at (125, 148). Just confirm the stacking
  and that pin 1 lands on J10 pin 1.
